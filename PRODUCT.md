# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Real-time news consumers (web and mobile application users) who want low-latency updates, customizable intelligence feeds, and semantic retrieval capabilities. They need highly readable, clean interfaces that allow them to digest news quickly and focus on what matters without clutter.

## Product Purpose
Imperium is an event-driven news platform featuring real-time ingestion, low-latency feeds, and vector-based semantic search. The frontend connects to Redis and Qdrant serving endpoints to deliver a fast, clean, and engaging news reading experience.

## Brand Personality
- **Sophisticated & Premium**: Clean editorial typography, subtle shadows, and high contrast.
- **Dynamic & Live**: Real-time intelligence, smooth transitions, and state changes that make the page feel responsive and alive.
- **Calm & Focused**: No heavy alerts or visual clutter. Tinted neutrals and single accent colors keep the cognitive load low.

## Anti-references
- **SaaS clichés**: Side-stripe border cards, standard neon gradients, hero-metric dashboards with tiny labels and giant numbers.
- **Ad-heavy portals**: Avoid crowded layouts, blinking banners, and complex nested card systems.

## Design Principles
1. **Editorial Focus**: Typography and spacing are paramount. Hierarchy should be distinct and line lengths kept readable.
2. **Micro-interactivity**: Every state transition or hover action should feel smooth and satisfying, utilizing exponential easing.
3. **Intentional Accents**: Color is used deliberately to highlight action items or status changes, keeping neutrals dominant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA contrast ratios using OKLCH and themed neutrals.
- Respect reduced motion settings (e.g., using Tailwind's `motion-safe:` or Framer Motion properties).
- Focus states and keyboard friendliness.
